How Outlook works with Albert

  1. Tap Connect Outlook and sign in with the DTS Microsoft account.
  2. Tap Check Outlook for jobs to see recent messages.
  3. Choose Draft reply for a potential customer or Make proposal for a job request.
  4. Review and edit every reply before tapping Send reply.
  5. Review and approve every proposal before tapping Email with Outlook.
  6. Replies and proposals appear in Outlook Sent Items.

Albert never replies automatically. A person must approve every customer message. Microsoft handles sign-in; Albert never stores your password.
